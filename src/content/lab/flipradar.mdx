FlipRadar evaluates Marktplaats listings for Apple Silicon Macs in real-time. It runs each listing through three LLMs in parallel, computes a consensus deal score, and generates a "Lazy Seller Index" — how likely the seller is to accept a lowball offer based on listing quality signals.
